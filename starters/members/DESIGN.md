# DESIGN.md

Fieldnote is a quiet, private notebook: it should feel calm, fast and trustworthy, like a well-made tool rather than a marketing site. Replace this plan with your own product's before changing the look.

## Palette

Light only. A cool off-white canvas (#f6f7f9), white surfaces, near-black ink (#121722) with two softer inks for secondary text, and one accent: ink blue (#2346d8) for buttons and links. Red is only for deleting, green only for success messages. Every pair meets WCAG AA.

## Type

Geist from Google Fonts, falling back to the system's sans serif. Headlines are weight 500 with tight tracking; body text is 16px at 1.6 line height. No serif, no all-caps labels.

## Layout

A slim top bar with the name and the account links. Signed-out pages are one centred card, 40rem wide at most. The home page splits into the promise on the left and a labelled sample notebook on the right, then three plain lines on how accounts are kept safe. The notes page is a compose box above a list. Everything stacks into one column on a phone, with 44px targets and 16px inputs.

## Signature

The sample notebook on the home page: a framed card of three real-looking notes with a small "Sample" tag, so people see what the app is for before they sign up.

## Motion

Almost none: buttons press down a pixel, and colour changes take 150ms. Nothing moves under reduced motion.
