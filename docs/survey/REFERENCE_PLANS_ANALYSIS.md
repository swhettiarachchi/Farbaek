# Reference plans: structure of real Sri Lankan survey plans

**What this is.** The owner shared photos of six survey plans prepared by a practising licensed surveyor in the Western Province (taken 2026-11-15). This document records their **structure only**, as the specification for Survey Studio's classic sheet (stage 191). It holds no names, plan numbers, places, dates or measurements from them (survey skill rules 2 and 14). Where an example is needed, it's a placeholder in angle brackets.

**Status.** This is how one practice draws plans: `observed in practice`. It isn't an official specification. `STANDARDS_RESEARCH.md` holds the official sources: the Survey Act and UDA regulation 9. Every caption and position below is a template setting the surveyor can change.

## Sheet layout (positions as shares of the frame, top left = 0, 0)

One variation: on the 14-lot plan the extent sits under the district and province, survey and partition dates are separate lines, and there are no bounded-as-follows lines on the face.

## Lettering

- **Style:** almost everything is a slanted (italic) single-stroke CAD lettering. "PLAN" and the district and province lines are upright.
- **Relative heights:** district and province (about 2%) > "PLAN" (1.8%) > plan number (1.5%) > lot labels (1.3%) > description (1.0%) > body and scale (0.9%) > drawing codes and legend (0.7 to 0.8%).
- **Emphasis:**
  - Land names are capitals in the description, sometimes bold.
  - Lot letters carry superscripts (a letter with a raised number, or a number with a raised letter).

## Drawing conventions

- **Boundaries:** solid lines, medium weight. Partition lines between lots are the same weight.
- **Lot labels:** inside each lot near its middle, slanted, larger than other drawing text, with a raised suffix. Reserved lots are labelled with the lot and explained in the description ("<n>(Res. for Access, <w>ft. wide)").
- **Boundary marks:** small slanted codes beside the corners and along the boundary, turned along the line. "CP" or "St" sit at corners, and "MW", "LF", "NF", "EDn" or "BFDn" sit along the leg they describe. A corner can carry two codes.
- **Roads:** two edge lines. The name is written along the road between them, with the width under it in brackets and in feet: "(<a>ft - <b>ft wide)" for a range, "(<w>ft. wide)" for one width. Ends beyond the survey stay open.
- **Direction texts:** "To Houses", "From <road>", placed along the road ends with an arrow line.
- **Adjoining land:** horizontal text outside the boundary.
  - Forms: "Land claimed by <name>", "Lot <x> in Plan No.<n>", "Lot <x> in my Plan No.<n> dated <d>" and "Remaining portion of lot <x> in Plan No.<n> dated <d> made by <name>, Licensed Surveyor".
  - Several lines when long, with an arrow leader when the text sits away from its leg.
- **Buildings:** outlines with "P" (permanent building) inside, or "T" for a toilet. "(To be demolished)" carries a leader. Named small structures are labelled in words (a shrine room with its local name in brackets, a bus halt).
- **Wells:** a small circle symbol with "Well".
- **Drains:**
  - "Proposed Drain" is a dashed line inside the boundary, labelled along it, with a flow arrow.
  - Existing earth drains are a line with ticks, coded "EDn".
- **Culverts:** "Cul" at the road.
- **Not on the face:** bearings, distances, coordinates, point numbers. They go in the "boundaries schedule" overleaf.

## The legend vocabulary seen (common usage, not an official list)

B Bank · BFDn Boundary Foundation · EDn Earth Drain · MDn Masonry Drain · MW Masonry Wall · RW Retaining Wall · LF Live Fence · NF Net Fence · CP Concrete Post · EP Electric Post · TP Telephone Post · St Stake · OSt Old Stake · M Mark · P Permanent Building · T Toilet · U Undefined · Cul Culvert

Each plan lists only the codes it uses, in no fixed order. Survey Studio ships these as editable defaults labelled "common usage, check".

## Title sentence grammar

After "PLAN", one sentence, centred:

```
of <count words> (<count 2 digits>) allotment(s) of land marked lot(s) <lot list>
called <LAND NAME> [alias <NAME> [and <NAME>]] [(Now called & known as <NAME>)]
being <operation clause>
situated at <village> Village in <pattu> of <korale>
```

- **Count:**
  - one lot is "an allotment of land marked lot <x>";
  - several are "<three> (03) allotments of land marked lots <x>, <y> & <z>";
  - reservations are written in the list: "<n>(Res. for Access, <w>ft. wide)".
- **Operation clauses seen:**
  - "resurvey of the existing boundaries & sub division of lot <x> depicted in Plan No.<n> dated <d> made by <name>, Licensed Surveyor";
  - "resurvey of the existing boundaries & amalgamation of lots <x> & <y> depicted in my Plan No.<n> dated <d>" ("my Plan" when it's the same surveyor's earlier plan);
  - per lot group: "Lot <x> being resurvey of … depicted in my Plan No.<n> dated <d>. Lots <list> being resurvey of the existing boundaries & sub division of remaining portion of lot <x> depicted in Plan No.<n> dated <d> made by <name>, Licensed Surveyor (Filed of record in <court>, Case No.<n>)".
- **Extracts** begin "of Lot <x> out of <two> (02) allotments of land marked lots <x> & <y> called …".

## Formats

| Item | Format seen |
| --- | --- |
| Extent | Hectares to 5 decimals; acres, roods and perches as "<a> A. <r> R. <pp.pp> P.", perches to 2 decimals and zero-padded to two whole digits. Four of the six agree with the international-yard perch (25.29285264 m²) to the last digit shown. One differs by 0.03 perch: the plan may compute the two from different areas, which Survey Studio never does |
| Dates | dd.mm.yyyy |
| Plan numbers | Free text: a number, a number with a letter, or "<number>/<year>" |
| Road widths | Feet, to the half foot, as a range or a single width |
| Counts | Words, with the number in two digits in brackets |

## What Survey Studio takes from this

- **The classic template:** portrait, every element above in the same place, slanted lettering, superscripted lot labels, the needle north arrow, the legend built from the codes used, the bounded-as-follows lines, and the extent in hectares and acres, roods and perches in these formats.
- **Several lots per plan,** with reservations and their notes.
- **Roads** from two surveyed edges, with the width range computed from them and written in feet.
- **Adjoining-land labels** with leaders, and direction texts.
- **The title sentence** composed from structured fields for each operation, never typed freehand by the software.
- **Bearings, distances and coordinates in the overleaf schedule,** not on the face. The panel layout from stage 190 stays available for surveyors who want them on the face.
- **Extracts,** without the "True Extract" words, which are the surveyor's own signed statement.
- **Not taken:** any name, number, place, date or measurement from these plans; handwritten notes; signatures and stamps (the sheet leaves room for them).
