# The homepage, measured against ChatGPT, Antigravity and Claude (2026-11-17)

Measured in Chrome with the pages' own computed styles, at 1920 and 1440 pixels wide. The owner ranked them: ChatGPT's overview first, Google Antigravity second, Claude third.

## What was wrong with Farbaek's homepage (stage 201's first pass)

1. **Too big.** An 83 px headline under an 84 px knight, a 28 px-cornered message box with the heaviest shadow, four bordered starter chips and three more links: five layers before the fold. ChatGPT has two.
2. **Cheap light.** A radial signal-blue pool behind the hero, a dot grid, a gradient under the last call. None of the three references uses one.
3. **A window of sketches.** The spaces window showed drawn placeholders (blank picture tiles, a dark rectangle for video) inside a raised shell with a knight on top: neither a real product nor a story.
4. **Generic rows.** Three equal icon cards for "one assistant", three stacked cards for safeguards, a five-column price grid: the patterns the redesign skill names as the most generic.
5. **Mixed alignment.** Some titles centred, the film's left with its meta on the right, the safeguards' sticky on the left.
6. **Heavy chrome.** A floating header island with a shadow; every card bordered and shadowed.

## The direction

Farbaek's own, built from what the three do best: ChatGPT's restraint and product-first story, Antigravity's lighter weight and "it's writing" motion, Claude's calm and its closing message box. Farbaek's identity stays: White Signal, Geist, signal blue as the one accent, the cat knight as the AI's face.

- **Canvas:** flat white. No dot grid, no radial pools, no gradient bands. Depth comes only from the product windows (one soft shadow) and flat light-grey bands (canvas-2, 24 px corners, no border, no shadow).
- **Type (Geist, weight 500, tight tracking):** hero 36 / 52 / 64 px (phone, tablet, desktop), line height 1.02; section titles 26 / 34 / 40 px, centred; card titles 17 px; body 15 to 17 px in ink-2. Nothing above 64 px.
- **Controls:** small. The header is a flat 64 px bar with 14 px links and one dark 40 px pill; buttons on the page are 40 px dark pills or text links with an arrow.
- **Hero:** two lines, a one-sentence lead, a quiet message box (white, hairline border, the lightest shadow, 24 px corners) with one row of plain starters under it. The knight moves into the product as Farbaek AI's face.
- **Product first:** under the hero, a large realistic Farbaek AI window with its sidebar (Chat, Images, Video, Build and recent chats) and a conversation that answers as you arrive: a heading, a short list and a table, drawn by the same Markdown styles the chat uses. It tilts gently into place in 3D as it scrolls in.
- **Tour:** ChatGPT's sticky list, for Farbaek's four spaces: Chat, Images, Video, Build, each with its promise, a paragraph and a link; one window on the right crossfades between their interfaces. On a phone each space stacks with its window.
- **One assistant:** a single band, not three cards: the words on the left, and on the right a drawing of a question going to Farbaek AI and on to the model for the job (writing, code, pictures, video), with the line lighting once as it comes into view.
- **Use cases:** ChatGPT's rail of tall flat cards with a real fragment of each space at the bottom, with arrows.
- **The rest:** the recorded build (centred title), "Built in, not bolted on" as two-column hairline cards with dark pills, Survey Studio and Proof as one quiet line, pricing as one flat band, and Claude's last word: "Not sure where to start? Ask Farbaek AI." with the message box again.
- **Motion:** only where it means something: the answer writing itself, the window settling into place, the tour's crossfade and progress line, the routing line lighting once. All under 500 ms per step except the answer's writing, and still under reduced motion.
